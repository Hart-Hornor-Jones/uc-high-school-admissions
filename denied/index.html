Admitted vs. denied — GPA and volume by school, campus and year

UC publishes, for every California high school × campus × year, the number of applicants and admits and the mean GPA of each. That is enough to recover the two populations an admission decision separates: the students a campus took and the students it turned away. Admitted is always blue, denied is always orange. Three views: a per-school timeline, the whole system on one screen, and every school at once. Denied counts and denied mean GPA are derived — exactly, from the published figures — as described under the caveats.

How “denied” is derived, and five caveats
  • Derivation. Denied volume = applicants − admits. Denied mean GPA is recovered by moment arithmetic: (Napp·GPAapp − Nadm·GPAadm) / Ndenied. UC publishes the applicant and admit means, so the denied mean is exact given those two.
  • Suppression. UC blanks small cells. Blank ≠ zero: missing years show as gaps, never as 0.
  • Small denominators. When fewer than 10 students were denied, the derived denied GPA is noise-dominated and is masked (shown as a gap) everywhere on this page. Derived values outside (0, 5) — a handful of tiny-denominator artifacts — are likewise blanked. The mask is applied when the dataset is built, not in the browser.
  • GPA coverage. Published means cover students with a UC-computable GPA; the derivation assumes similar coverage among admitted and denied.
  • Campus-label repair. The source GPA tab lists Universitywide first, so its raw campus labels are shifted one position; this page uses the corrected assignment (verified: the corrected systemwide GPA matches the unrepaired “Berkeley” column in 29,131 of 29,131 school-years, and post-repair admit-GPA coverage tracks campus selectivity).
  • Universe. 1,536 California public high schools — the same school list and naming as the rest of the site. Community-college and unidentifiable source codes that ride along in the counts extract are excluded (they carry 69 of 129,625 displayable denied-GPA school-years).

The wedge timeline

Each year is one vertical dumbbell from the denied mean GPA up to the admitted mean GPA. Dot area is the number of students on each side, so a glyph reads: how high the bar, how wide the gap, how many taken, how many turned away. Hover for exact values.

The wall

Rows are campuses (systemwide on top), each split into an admitted and a denied strip; columns are the 32 years; cell darkness is mean GPA on a single blue ramp, so the two strips compare directly. Blank cells are data — suppressed or masked, never zero. The right margin totals 2015–2025 volumes.

The field

Zooming out: every California public high school applying to the selected campus in a year — admitted mean GPA against denied mean GPA, dot area = applicants, schools with fewer than 10 denials not shown. The diagonal is “admitted = denied”; height above it is the school's wedge. The selected school is highlighted; click any dot to select that school.
