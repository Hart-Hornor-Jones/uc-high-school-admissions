Admitted vs. denied — GPA and volume by school, campus and year

UC publishes, for every California high school × campus × year, the number of applicants and admits and the mean GPA of each. That is enough to recover the two populations an admission decision separates: the students a campus took and the students it turned away. Admitted is always blue, denied is always orange. The page opens on all California public high schools pooled; type a school name to narrow every view to that one school. Denied counts and denied mean GPA are derived — exactly, from the published figures; the derivation and six caveats are at the foot of the page.

Campus applies to the timeline and the scatter; the grid always shows all campuses.
Admitted and denied GPA by year

Each year is one vertical bar running from the denied mean GPA up to the admitted mean GPA. Dot area is the number of students on each side, so a single mark reads: how high the GPA, how wide the gap, how many taken, how many turned away. Hover for exact values.

How “denied” is derived, and six caveats
  • Derivation. Denied volume = applicants − admits. Denied mean GPA is recovered by moment arithmetic: (Napp·GPAapp − Nadm·GPAadm) / Ndenied. UC publishes the applicant and admit means, so the denied mean is exact given those two.
  • Suppression. UC blanks small cells. Blank ≠ zero: missing years show as gaps, never as 0.
  • Small denominators. When fewer than 10 students were denied, the derived denied GPA is noise-dominated and is masked (shown as a gap) everywhere on this page. Derived values outside (0, 5) — a handful of tiny-denominator artifacts — are likewise blanked. The mask is applied when the dataset is built, not in the browser.
  • The pooled “all high schools” figures. With the school box empty, each campus-year shows one weighted average over every California public high school whose applicant and admit GPA UC published that year: admitted mean = Σ(Nadm·GPAadm) / ΣNadm, and the denied mean is the same moment identity applied to the pooled totals. Because it is applied to totals, the pooled denied mean is not affected by the small-denominator mask — schools masked individually still contribute. The pooled counts are the counts of those contributing schools (shown on hover), not UC’s campus grand total, since a school-year whose GPA is suppressed cannot enter a GPA-weighted mean.
  • GPA coverage. Published means cover students with a UC-computable GPA; the derivation assumes similar coverage among admitted and denied.
  • Campus-label repair. The source GPA tab lists Universitywide first, so its raw campus labels are shifted one position; this page uses the corrected assignment (verified: the corrected systemwide GPA matches the unrepaired “Berkeley” column in 29,131 of 29,131 school-years, and post-repair admit-GPA coverage tracks campus selectivity).
  • Universe. 1,536 California public high schools — the same school list and naming as the rest of the site. Community-college and unidentifiable source codes that ride along in the counts extract are excluded (they carry 69 of 129,625 displayable denied-GPA school-years).
