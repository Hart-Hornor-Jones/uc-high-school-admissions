Measures

Reading the by-campus completion panel

Federal C150 rates are keyed here to the Scorecard data year in which they were published. Each value reflects a cohort that entered roughly six years earlier; comparisons across campuses or groups within a year are unaffected, but do not read the x-axis as entering-cohort year.

GPA bands

Bands are terciles of each campus × cohort's own enrollee GPA distribution (weighted-capped high-school GPA for freshman entrants, transfer GPA for transfer entrants), so band boundaries differ by campus and year — the boundaries themselves are shown under the chart and in tooltips. For freshman entrants the source publishes graduate counts per band but no entering-cohort denominators, so graduation rates per band are not identified; the chart instead shows how graduates split by time to degree. For transfer entrants the source also publishes the band-level completion rates, shown directly (values are integer-rounded at source; cumulative rates are sums of rounded parts).

Masking & small groups

School-level cells are shown by the source only when the entering cohort has at least 10 students, as integer percents. GPA-band timing shares are hidden here when fewer than 25 graduates underlie them. Systemwide lines carry the published cohort sizes in tooltips; small groups (for example American Indian entrants) are noisy — read them with their Ns.

Cohorts of 2020 and 2021

Entering cohorts 2020–2021 experienced pandemic disruption, and 2021 was the first test-blind cohort; 2020–21 high-school records also carry grade inflation. Charts shade these cohorts. Recent cohorts also have short follow-up: a six-year rate exists only for cohorts entering by 2019.

What these data cannot show

Individual students' grades, major switching, or reasons for leaving; anything about students below the published masking thresholds; and for Scorecard measures, anyone outside the federal-aid universe.
